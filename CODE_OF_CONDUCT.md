# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it. Reports should be made privately to the repository maintainer through the contact route on their GitHub profile.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
